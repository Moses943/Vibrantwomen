Who are Vibrant Women?

Who we are

Vibrant Women is a community of women who support, inspire and uplift one another in faith, family, business and service.

What we do

We hold fellowship meetings, mentorship and skills training, savings and business groups, and outreach to families in need.

Our Vision & Plan

Vision

A generation of women who are confident, financially independent and leaders in their homes and communities.

Plan

Grow membership in every province, run training and savings groups, host yearly conferences and support women-led enterprises.
